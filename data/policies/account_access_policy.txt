ACCOUNT ACCESS AND GENERAL ACCOUNT OPERATIONS POLICY

1. NET BANKING ACCESS RECOVERY

1.1 Net banking access is temporarily suspended after 3 failed login attempts.

1.2 Customers can regain access through any of the following:
    - Generate a new IPIN at an ATM.
    - Call the bank's IVR helpline.
    - Visit the nearest branch with valid ID proof.

1.3 Access is restored immediately after a successful PIN reset.


2. SALARY CREDIT DELAYS

2.1 Salary credit delays may occur because of:
    - Employer payroll batch timing.
    - Bank holidays.

2.2 Customers should first check with their HR or payroll team
    whether the salary payment has been processed.

2.3 If the employer confirms that the payment has been processed
    but the amount has not been credited by end of day,
    the customer may raise a complaint.

2.4 The bank can then trace the NEFT/RTGS transaction.


3. JOINT ACCOUNT OPENING

3.1 A joint account can be opened by both account holders
    visiting the branch.

3.2 Required documents include:
    - PAN
    - Aadhaar
    - Passport-size photographs

3.3 Available operating modes include:
    - Either or Survivor
    - Former or Survivor
    - Jointly

3.4 The account-opening process takes approximately
    1-2 working days.
